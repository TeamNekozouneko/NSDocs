## About Merging
We will merge the Survival server into the Life server for the following reasons.
* (Operational) 
The Survival server was not designed with ease of operation in mind, it requires higher server performance than the Life server, and its TPS* can drop below 10 with as few as 20 concurrent players. In contrast, the Life server maintains a TPS of nearly 20 even with around 70 concurrent players.
::: tip What is the TPS?
The indicates how frequently the server can process requests. Using 20 as a baseline, the lower the value, the longer it takes to process requests.
(This is different from Ping.)
:::
* (Our Policy)  
The Life server prioritizes freedom and lightweight experience. Eventually, the Life server will likely incorporate elements and features from the Survival server.
Based on our policy to eventually allow players to enjoy Survival server elements on the Life server, we have decided to proceed with the merger.

## Details of the merging
* (Regarding Your Data)  
Buildings, Items and Entities can be transferred to the Life server. We will explain the transfer process later.
::: tip BY THE WAY

(However, entities inside your house will be transferred at the same time.)

* Entities include armor stands, item frames, and immersive paintings.
:::
* (After The Merge)  
Once the full merge is complete, the Survival server **will be shutdown**. Please use the Life server after the merge.

Please note the coordinates and landmarks of the location you want to transfer to.

Please fill out the form based on the coordinates and landmarks you noted. If you cannot fit everything into a single form, it's okay to submit it in multiple parts.
6. (Submit the form)  
Your application is complete once you click the Submit button.

## Frequently Asked Questions
::: info My application was rejected. Can I resubmit?
Yes, even if your application is rejected, you can resubmit as many times as you like. However, if you do not review the reason for rejection and improve your application, it may continue to be rejected.
:::

::: info Can I submit using a sub account?
Yes, you can submit using a sub account. However, if your IGN you enter does not match the building's owner, your application may be rejected. Please include your all IGNs in the field.
:::

::: info Can I submit on behalf of someone else?
Yes, you can submit on behalf of someone else. However, if your IGN enter does not match the building's owner, your application may be rejected. Please include the substitute's IGN.

Additionally, when submitting on behalf of someone else, you must obtain permission from the building's owner.
:::

::: info There are multiple owners within the same building.
For example, if a house is a single building but has different owners for each room, you can migrate the entire buildings as long as you obtain permission from all parties involved.

However, if your IGN enter does not match the building's owner, your application may be rejected. Please include `(Everyone Agreed)` in the IGN field.
:::
